## About ClaviOpen

ClaviOpen is an open-source initiative dedicated to making privacy more accessible.

We believe that people shouldn't have to sacrifice privacy to benefit from useful digital tools. Our goal is to build open-source projects that enhance existing tools and workflows with privacy-preserving features, giving users greater control over their information.

Projects such as **Clavimit** and **Clavidoc** put this philosophy into practice.

We welcome contributions from anyone who shares our vision of a more privacy-conscious digital world.
